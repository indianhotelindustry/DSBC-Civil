**Invariant:** No business rules, workflows, financial calculations, Firestore rules, collection names, routes, or API endpoints were changed.

---

## 1. Forensic inventory (Phase 0 summary)

150 occurrences of `SIPL`/`sipl` were found across 44 files (excluding `node_modules`). They fall into two fundamentally different classes:

- **Product branding** ("SIPL Work Orders", "SIPL Portal", package name `sipl-work-orders`) — **renamed**.
- **Domain/business identifiers** — `SIPL` is an operating **company** inside the ERP's data model (`CompanyCode = 'SIPL' | 'SHSPL'`, seeded company ids `'sipl'`/`'shspl'`, the "SIPL-only" Sales/Recovery/Customers module semantics, test fixtures, print-document company names). These live in Firestore data and business rules — **intentionally NOT renamed**.

A prior partial rebranding to "DSBC ERP Platform" already existed in README/governance docs; this pass aligned everything to the official product name **DSBC Civil — Enterprise Construction ERP Platform**.
